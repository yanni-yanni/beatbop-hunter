A multi-band complex-domain onset detector accumulates the residual |X − m1·exp(j(2φ1−φ2))| into six frequency bands. Each band runs its own median/MAD picker plus a running-max activity gate, so an idle band can't manufacture onsets from its noise floor. Everything here is purely reactive.
